Privacy Policy — PikaEdit

Effective Date: November 2026

PikaEdit is a code editor and ZIP project runner built for mobile and web users.

1. Local Data & Files

All HTML, CSS, JS files, images, and ZIP projects you create or import in PikaEdit are stored locally on your own device (via browser IndexedDB/LocalStorage). We do not upload or read your personal code files on any external server.

2. Advertisements & Third-Party Services

The app may display third-party advertisements (such as Adsterra or Monetag) and load open-source libraries (such as JSZip) to provide ZIP functionality. Third-party ad networks may use standard non-personal device identifiers or cookies to show relevant ads.

3. Children's Privacy

PikaEdit does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
