🛡️PikaEdit Privacy Policy

1. 100% Local & Offline-First Storage

PikaEdit stores all your HTML, CSS, JavaScript, ZIP archives, images, and audio assets strictly on your device using your browser's local IndexedDB storage. Your code and project files are never uploaded to any external PikaEdit server.

2. Zero Tracking & No Account Required

We do not require sign-ups, logins, or personal details. PikaEdit does not collect analytics, keystrokes, or personal identifiers from your workspace sections.

3. Virtual File System (VFS) & Live Runner

When you tap Run or Open in Device Browser, PikaEdit compiles your project locally inside your browser using in-memory Data URLs and Blob URLs. External libraries (such as JSZip from cdnjs) are fetched only when needed to extract or bundle ZIP archives.

4. Complete User Control

You can export any section or folder as a `.zip` file anytime, or wipe individual files, folders, or sections instantly using swipe gestures, multi-select, or Reset.
