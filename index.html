🛡️PikaEdit Privacy Policy

1. 100% Local & Offline-First Storage

PikaEdit stores all your HTML, CSS, JavaScript, ZIP archives, images, and audio assets strictly on your device using your browser's local IndexedDB storage. Your code and project files are never uploaded to any external server.

2. Zero Tracking & No Account Required

No sign-ups, logins, or personal details are required. PikaEdit does not collect analytics, keystrokes, or personal identifiers.

3. Virtual File System (VFS) & Live Runner

When you tap Run or Open in Device Browser, PikaEdit compiles your project locally inside your browser using in-memory Data URLs and Blob URLs.

4. Complete User Control

You can download individual files, export a section as a `.zip` file anytime, or remove files, folders, and sections using swipe gestures, multi-select, or Reset.
